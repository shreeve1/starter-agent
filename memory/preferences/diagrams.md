## Active memory

- Use flowcharts and diagrams as much as reasonably possible, in every chat: day-to-day answers as well as design sessions. Their purpose is shared understanding between the owner and the agent, not decoration.
- Draw one whenever a reply describes a flow, a sequence of steps, a decision path, how parts relate, or a before and after. Use Mermaid in chat.
- In a talk-it-through or design session, open with a diagram of the plan as understood and redraw it when an answer changes its shape.
- Skip a diagram for a single fact or a one-step answer.
