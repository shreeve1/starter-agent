# Interview

Ask these through `talk-it-through`, one at a time, each with your recommended answer. Skip any the owner already answered. Twelve questions at most; stop early when the rest have obvious defaults, and state the default you used.

A blank or vague answer counts as not answered (core rule 6). Ask again once, then use the recommended default and say so.

## Questions

1. **What is a work item for you?** Tickets, email threads, tasks in a to-do app, projects, or a mix? What makes one item separate from another?
   Recommend: the thing you already track to completion.

2. **Where do items come from?** Which tool or connector holds them (help desk, mail, task app, spreadsheet, calendar)? Can your agent already read it here?
   Recommend: the one source you check first every morning.

3. **Which items are yours today?** Assigned to you, flagged, due soon, in one folder or list? Anything to always skip?

4. **Working hours and timezone?** When should the loop start, and when should it stop starting new research?
   Recommend: stop new research an hour before your day ends.

5. **What does "done for the day" mean?** A time, an empty list, or both? What summary do you want at the end?

6. **What may the agent read?** Mail, calendar, files, the web, other systems? Anything strictly off limits?

7. **What counts as a change needing your approval?** List the actions you care about (replies, closing items, moving files, booking time). Everything else that writes or sends is a change too.
   Recommend: anything that sends, deletes, moves, books or edits outside the research files.

8. **Where should research files and the approvals page live?** A folder in this workspace, a notes app, a shared drive?
   Recommend: a folder in this workspace, one file per item, plus one approvals page.

9. **How do you want to be told something needs you?** Desktop notification, chat message, email to yourself, or just the approvals page?
   Recommend: one quiet notification per batch, no private details in it.

10. **Which agent app or harness do you run, on which machine?** This decides how the loop repeats during the day (see `adaptation-list.md`).

11. **How often should the loop check for new activity?** Every few minutes, every half hour, only when you nudge it?
    Recommend: every 15 minutes during working hours.

12. **Anything that must never happen?** People never to contact, items never to touch, times never to send.

## After the interview

Restate the answers as a short list. Ask "Is anything here wrong?" Fix it, then fill in `skeleton-SKILL.md`.
